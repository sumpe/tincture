# The Tincture Project

Grammar drills games.

- **[Polyverb](polyverb/)**: verb conjugation in Portuguese, Polish and Swedish.
- **[Casebook](casebook/)**: noun plurals, cases and gender in Portuguese, Polish and Swedish.
- **[Metaphrase](metaphrase/)**: translating sentences from English into European or Brazilian Portuguese.

Nothing to install: open `index.html` in a browser.

Site: https://sumpe.github.io/tincture/

## History

This repo was `sumpe/polyverb`, renamed. Casebook began as Polynoun, in
`sumpe/polynoun`; its history was merged in with its commit IDs unchanged.
Metaphrase began here as Polyphrase. Both were renamed in November 2026.
